Cosmic Web

The nearby universe, drawn from 43 thousand real galaxies in the 2MASS Redshift Survey; deeper out, 600 thousand DESI galaxies and quasars trace the web out to z≈3.5. Known clusters, walls and voids farther out sit at their measured positions. Everywhere not yet mapped, including the region behind the Milky Way, is filled with a guess that mimics the statistics of the real web.

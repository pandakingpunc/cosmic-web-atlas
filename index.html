Cosmic Web

The nearby universe, drawn from 43 thousand real galaxies in the 2MASS Redshift Survey. Known clusters, walls and voids farther out sit at their measured positions. Everywhere not yet mapped, including the region behind the Milky Way, is filled with a guess that mimics the statistics of the real web.
